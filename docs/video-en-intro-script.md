# English Intro Video Script — "Bannerlord won't let you use half its tactics. So I talk to it."

> 写法:按 youtube-scriptwriting 那套方法重写 —— 先定观众/情绪/承诺,挑"惊讶分"高的事实,
> 钩子确认点击并开一个悬念,正文每 30~60 秒一次 rehook,次好的点放前、最好的点放后,
> 结尾用 binge loop。口播全部是短句、主动语态、六年级词汇,方便非母语念稳。
> VO = 你念的英文;括号里是中文的镜头/操作提示;所有指令用词典第一别名(识别最稳)。

## Pre-script planning

- **Target audience**: Bannerlord players who already command formations and hate juggling F-keys mid-fight. They know what a shield wall is. They don't know the game has an AI layer.
- **Desired emotion**: surprise → "wait, it can do *that*?" (次要:痛快)
- **Core promise**: In five minutes you'll see an army obey your voice, do things the keyboard physically can't, and learn it's free.
- **Title keyword**: Bannerlord voice command

**High-shock facts (惊讶分)**
1. (90) Every formation has an AI brain with tactics — hold high ground, skirmish, cautious advance — and the player is never allowed to trigger them. Only the enemy general gets to use them.
2. (85) You can't split a formation mid-battle from the keyboard. The game has 8 slots; you just can't reach them.
3. (80) If you delegate a formation to the AI (F6), the *team* AI can quietly merge your infantry and archers into one blob. This mod runs tactics without delegating.
4. (75) The whole thing runs offline on your PC, and answers in about 0.2 seconds.
5. (70) "Cavalry, charge their archers" is a real API lock-on, not a fake cursor.
6. (60) It learned a Chinese accent. "All units" heard as "Or unit" still charges.

## BODY 1 — The basics, hands-free (0:15–1:15)

**Rehook**: "Let's start with the part every Bannerlord player has wanted since 2020."

VO:
> "I'm on a horse. Both hands are busy. Watch the top of the screen."

(连喊,每条给横幅 1 秒,不解说)
> **"Infantry, shield wall."** → **"Archers, spread out."** → **"Cavalry, wedge."** → **"All units, advance."** → **"Archers, fire at will."** → **"Archers, hold fire."**

VO:
> "Six orders. Zero keys. About a fifth of a second each.
> And that's the boring part."

**Mini-payoff**: 字幕 "That was the boring part." 直接切下一段。

## BODY 2 — Orders the keyboard can't give (1:15–2:30)  ← 次好的点

**Rehook**: "Here's where it stops being a voice remote and starts being a new controller."

**Context**: "With F-keys you can tell cavalry to charge. You can't tell them *who* to charge."

**Application**:
> **"Cavalry, charge their archers!"**
> (横幅特写 `Cavalry → attacking enemy Archers (N)`;跟拍骑兵绕过步兵直扑弓箭手)

VO: "That's not a cursor trick. The mod locks my cavalry onto their archer formation through the game's own code."

> **"Cavalry, attack them!"**
> (混战中,骑兵扑向离你最近的那支)

VO: "In a melee I don't know what that unit is called. I say *attack them*. It hits whatever is closest to me."

> **"Cavalry, split!"** → 横幅 `Cavalry split in two: Left = original, Right = Group 5`
> **"Cavalry left, charge their archers!"** → **"Group five, follow me!"**

VO: "Two wings. Two jobs. One breath. There is no key for this. The game has the slots — it just never gave you the button."

**Framing / mini-payoff**: "So far, faster hands. Now the part I actually built this for."

---

## BODY 3 — The AI layer (2:30–4:00)  ← 最好的点

**Rehook**: "Every formation in this game has a brain. You've been fighting it for years. You've never been allowed to use it."

**Context**:
> "The AI has behaviors: hold the high ground, skirmish, advance under archer cover, guard a flank. The enemy general calls them every battle.
> Your only option is F6 — hand the whole formation over. And when you do, the team AI can merge your infantry and archers into one blob. I learned that the hard way."

(可插 3 秒花絮:步弓被合并成一团的实测画面,字幕 "delegation: never again")

**Application**(每条一个镜头,横幅特写):
> **"Archers, hold the high ground!"** (俯视镜头看弓箭手自己爬坡)
> **"Cavalry, protect the left flank!"** (骑兵站到弓箭手左翼;敌骑逼近时自动冲出去再回来 —— 这个来回是本段最好的画面)
> **"Infantry, advance carefully!"** (盾墙贴着弓箭手射程慢推)
> **"Horse archers, skirmish!"** (骑射放风筝)

VO(收回演示):
> "And the formation is still mine. Watch."
> (对弓箭手按一下 F1,或喊 **"Archers, halt!"**)
> "One direct order, and it snaps back. No delegation. No losing your army to the AI."

**Framing**: "This is the layer the game kept for itself. Now it takes orders."

---

## BODY 4 — Setup + three honest things (4:00–5:00)

**Rehook**: "You're wondering what the catch is. There are three, and none of them is money."

(屏幕录制配字幕:Subscribe → Mods 勾选 → Play → 两个弹窗 → 选麦克风)

VO:
> "Setup is one Workshop subscribe. Link below.
> First launch shows two popups: an *unverified code* warning, because the mod opens a local port; and a UAC prompt, because it presses keys for you. Both are expected.
> Catch one: English support is new. I'm a Chinese streamer; this started as a Chinese mod. Run the calibration once — you read every command out loud and it learns your accent. It learned mine.
> Catch two: it's better with an NVIDIA card. One click downloads the accurate model.
> Catch three: it's unlisted for now. I want feedback before I go public."

(花絮 5 秒:"All units" 被听成 "Or unit" 照样冲锋,字幕 "it forgives my accent")
